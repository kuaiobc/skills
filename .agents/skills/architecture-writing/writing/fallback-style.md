# Minimal Fallback Style

Use only when the separate `writing-style` Skill is unavailable.

1. Prefer concrete statements over generic introductions.
2. State the conclusion or question early.
3. Keep one main idea per paragraph.
4. Separate facts, assumptions, judgments, and recommendations.
5. Explain trade-offs rather than declaring a solution universally best.
6. Avoid repetitive AI-style transitions and empty summary sentences.
7. Avoid invented personal experiences or fake anecdotes.
8. Keep headings useful; do not manufacture sections merely to make the document look structured.
9. Preserve uncertainty explicitly.
10. Do not sacrifice technical precision for conversational tone.
