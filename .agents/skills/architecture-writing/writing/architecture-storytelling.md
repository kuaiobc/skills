# Architecture Storytelling

Architecture writing is causal explanation, not component cataloging.

Recommended narrative:
1. context and business intent
2. problem and why now
3. requirements and constraints
4. architecture drivers
5. current-state evidence
6. architectural gaps
7. alternatives
8. trade-offs
9. recommendation
10. human decision
11. consequences and risks
12. validation
13. migration and evolution

SCQA is useful for compressing this narrative for executives, but detailed architecture documents should retain the causal chain.
