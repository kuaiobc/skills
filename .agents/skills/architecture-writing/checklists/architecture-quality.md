# Architecture Quality Checklist

- [ ] Problem and goals are explicit
- [ ] Requirements and constraints are traceable
- [ ] Drivers are prioritized
- [ ] Current and target states are modeled
- [ ] Boundaries and ownership are justified
- [ ] Critical flows and failure modes are covered
- [ ] Quality scenarios have measures/targets
- [ ] Alternatives and trade-offs are explicit
- [ ] Decision consequences and risks are recorded
- [ ] Security and trust boundaries are considered
- [ ] Operations and observability are designed
- [ ] Migration and rollback are feasible
- [ ] Diagrams match the model
- [ ] Unknowns have validation actions
