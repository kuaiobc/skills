# SCQA

## Purpose
Structure a complex architecture narrative so the reader understands context, tension, question, and answer.

## Contract
Input: situation, complication, question, evidence, decision candidate.
Output: concise narrative with explicit answer.

## Rules
- Situation is context, not justification.
- Complication explains why the status quo is insufficient.
- Question must be decision-relevant.
- Answer must not contain claims unsupported by the analysis.

SCQA is normally a synthesis method and should follow substantive analysis when used for architecture decisions.
