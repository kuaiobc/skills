# Reasoning Pipeline

```text
Question
  ↓
Objective
  ↓
Problem Classification
  ↓
Evidence Inventory
  ↓
Method Selection
  ↓
Method Execution
  ↓
Intermediate Results
  ↓
Cross-check / Contradiction Review
  ↓
Synthesis
  ↓
Decision Candidate
  ↓
Decision Validation
```

Every step should record its input and output. If an intermediate result is inferred rather than directly observed, label its confidence.
