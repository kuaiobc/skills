# Reasoning Quality

Assess each reasoning run on:

- Relevance: does the method answer the stated question?
- Evidence: can material claims be traced?
- Completeness: are decision-critical dimensions covered?
- Consistency: are categories, criteria, and scales applied consistently?
- Reproducibility: can another reviewer reproduce the result?
- Uncertainty: are unknowns visible?
- Independence: are criteria accidentally double-counting the same property?
- Decision traceability: does the decision follow from the analysis?

A polished diagram or score table is not evidence of reasoning quality.
