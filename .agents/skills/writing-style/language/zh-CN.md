# zh-CN

- Prefer idiomatic contemporary Simplified Chinese; allow omitted subjects
  where context is clear.
- Avoid mechanically copying English subject-verb-object order, connective
  chains, excessive nominalization, and translationese.
- Vary sentence length and paragraph rhythm to serve the argument.
- Preserve established English technical terms when they are standard in the
  field; explain them when the audience needs it.
- Do not add bureaucratic phrasing merely to sound formal.
- Use direct claims and explicit reasoning without forcing every relationship
  into a transition word.
