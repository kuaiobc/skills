# Final Critic

Seven questions:

1. Is every major claim supported by the provided material or clearly qualified?
2. Is there a real judgment, trade-off, observation, or implication?
3. Did the style improve the document rather than decorate it?
4. Does anything sound generated mainly because it is a familiar AI pattern?
5. Did the text avoid fabricated experience?
6. Would a real reader know what to think, understand, decide, or do next?
7. Can any paragraph be deleted without meaningful loss?

If the last answer is yes, delete or rewrite it.
